Sex & intimacy coaching

Embodied, direct, judgment-free support for your sex life and your relationships, built around you.

What is sex & intimacy coaching?

Coaching is forward-focused, hands-on support for the sex life and relationships you want. Instead of only talking about what's in the way, we work with your body, your desires, and your relationships in real time.

My approach is somatic, attachment-focused, and experiential. That means we pay attention to what you feel, not just what you think, and you leave each session with practices you can actually use.

It's not therapy. I don't diagnose, give medical advice, or provide trauma work, and I'm glad to collaborate with your therapist or healthcare providers.

What we work on

Individuals, couples, polycules, and all non-traditional relationship structures are welcome.

Eroticism & desire

Connect with your eroticism, whether it's brand new or you've drifted away from it.

Kink & BDSM

Explore power, play, and new roles like dominance or submission, with clarity and care.

Non-monogamy

Open a relationship, navigate a polycule, or get support after years of non-monogamy.

Sexual function

Work through sexual concerns, performance anxiety, and a sex life that could feel better.

Embodiment

Build body literacy so you know what you feel and what you want, and can access new pleasure.

Life transitions

Find your way back to pleasure after changes that have shifted your sexuality.

What a session looks like

Every session is 1:1, virtual, and 75 minutes.

01

Arrive & check in

We start with where you are right now: what's present in your body, your relationships, and your goals.

02

Explore & practice

Direct conversation paired with body-based work, so insight becomes something you actually feel.

03

Take it home

You leave with tailored practices to try between sessions, and we build on them together next time.

What to expect from me

  • Warmth and directness. I'll name what I see, kindly and clearly.
  • Support and accountability. I'll hold you, and I'll push you with kindness.
  • Your pace, your choices. You're part of every decision we make.
  • No shame, no judgment. Whatever you bring is welcome here.
