About

Hi, I'm Claire.

I'm a somatic sex and intimacy coach, and I help people step into their most authentic sexual selves.

Why I do this work

Sex and intimacy have never been places of shame or taboo for me. They've been one of my great passions, and I wanted to take that passion and give it back to the world as a source of open-minded education on sex, intimacy, polyamory, and non-monogamy.

When I went looking for support around sex, kink, and polyamory myself, traditional talk therapy wasn't enough. I didn't feel supported, and I didn't find much real competence in these areas. I wanted something more experiential and direct, with someone who would hold me every step of the way.

That's what I offer now: warm, direct, transformational support that helps you build pleasure, connect to your eroticism, and feel held, supported, and challenged along the way.

How I work

Somatic, attachment-focused, and experiential, grounded in evidence-based tools and tailored to you.

Body first

Somatic

We work with what your body is actually feeling, not just what you think about it, so you can build real literacy around who you are and how you feel.

Relationship-aware

Attachment-focused

How we connect shapes how we desire. We look at the patterns underneath your intimacy, whether you're solo, partnered, or part of a polycule.

Hands-on

Experiential

Expect practices and experiments, not just conversation. I'll push you with kindness and hold you accountable, and you'll be part of every decision we make.

Who I work with

I work with individuals, couples, polycules, and all non-traditional relationship structures. My clients tend to be self-motivated people, often leaders in the rest of their lives, who treat sex and intimacy as an important part of who they are.

People often come to me wanting to:

  • Connect with their eroticism, whether it's brand new or they've drifted from it
  • Step into new roles in kink and BDSM, like dominance or submission
  • Navigate life changes that have shifted their sexuality
  • Open a relationship, or get support after years of non-monogamy
  • Work through sexual concerns, function, or performance anxiety
  • Feel more at home in their bodies and access new kinds of pleasure

Training & background

My training lets me bring clinical depth and clear communication to every session.

  • Trained in marriage & family therapy and sex therapy
  • Numerous trainings in somatic sexuality
  • Registered yoga & mindfulness instructor
  • Kink, BDSM & alternative relationship styles

Coaching, not therapy

Though I'm trained as a therapist, at Sensoria I work as a coach. I don't diagnose, give medical advice, or provide trauma work. I offer short-term and ongoing coaching, and I'm happy to collaborate with your therapist or healthcare providers.
